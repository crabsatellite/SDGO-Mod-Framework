# SDGO Mod Manager 1.16.64

## 中文

- 修复 AI 在 U.E REEF 一类宇宙地图上把空域当成地面行走的问题。空中路线继续接受原版碰撞与地图边界检查。
- AI 落入原生死亡区时按原版规则判死和扣命。AI 复活后能正常行动，再次落井也会判死。

## English

- Fixed AI navigation on space maps such as U.E REEF. Short routes through open air retain native collision and map boundary checks.
- AI falling into a native death zone uses the original death and life-cost flow. Respawned AI can move and die from another fall.
