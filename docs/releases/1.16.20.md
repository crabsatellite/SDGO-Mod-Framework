## 中文

导入任务支持加载海报、地面边界线和设计图掉落；任务 AI 僚机放必杀时，房主的副官不再替它发言。

- 任务可选择专属加载海报或原版随机加载画面。
- 地图边界与任务阶段区域在地面画出与原版对战地图相同的黄色边界线，可在地图包中关闭。
- 任务可配置结算掉落：保留同等级原版掉落，并按每 1000 次通关的概率掉落任务中出现机体的设计图。
- 有名 NPC 可使用独立的名称与攻击倍率；过场中的角色可悬停在原位，AI 僚机不会在过场期间攻击。
- 修复导入任务在第 7 阶段及以后有人阵亡复活时客户端崩溃：任务地形为每个阶段生成一对复活点。
- `battle_ai`：AI 驾驶员的必杀不再触发房主副官的必杀台词。

## English

Imported missions gain loading posters, ground boundary lines and blueprint drops; the host's adjutant no longer speaks for AI wingmen's specials.

- A mission can show its own loading poster or keep the stock random loading screen.
- Map and mission-phase boundaries are painted on the ground as yellow lines like the stock PvP maps; packages can turn them off.
- Missions can configure end-of-mission drops: keep the stock drops of the same level and add blueprints of units that appear in the mission at a per-1000-clears chance.
- Named NPCs take their own label and attack factor; cutscene actors can hold their position, and AI wingmen do not attack during cutscenes.
- Fixes a client crash when a pilot respawned in phase 7 or later of an imported mission: the mission terrain now has a respawn pair for every phase.
- `battle_ai`: AI pilots' specials no longer trigger the host adjutant's special-attack lines.
