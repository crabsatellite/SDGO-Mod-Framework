## 中文

修复多目标光束的两段命中收集共用结果区时，后续伤害写入越过原生容量的问题。

- 排序后的机体命中循环沿用原生 12 槽上限，满槽后进入原有收尾，保留容量以内的目标顺序和伤害计算。
- 保护发生在写入前，保留寄存器、标志位及浮点状态；不修改机体、技能、武器或已经生成的伤害值。
- 已验证真实保护触发及完整战斗、结算和大厅返回；新增实际 DLL 的边界与栈保护测试和只读触发计数。
- 技能导入指南补充多目标武器的验证范围。私有机体、素材与观测插件不随发布包分发，更新后重启客户端生效。

## English

Fixes damage writes exceeding the native result-bank capacity when both multi-target beam hit passes share that bank.

- The sorted actor pass follows the existing twelve-slot limit and original finish path, preserving in-range target order and damage calculation.
- The check precedes the write and preserves registers, flags and floating-point state. It does not edit units, skills, weapons or previously calculated damage.
- Verified real guard activation and a complete battle, settlement and lobby return. Adds actual-DLL boundary and stack-cookie tests plus a read-only activation counter.
- Extends the skill-import guide with multi-target weapon checks. Private units, assets and observation plugins are not bundled. Restart the client after updating.
