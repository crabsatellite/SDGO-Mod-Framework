## 中文

GameTest 修复：隐藏桌面上的原生客户端在战斗开始时不再卡在显卡状态检查上。

- GameTest 把客户端放在隐藏桌面上运行。画面提交正常时，测试层直接回答显卡状态检查，不再交给后台图形线程，因为战斗开始时加载线程可能占着设备锁，同时在等游戏主线程。
- 之前只有提交结果恰好是 D3D_OK 时才走这条直接回答的路径；隐藏桌面上常见的“窗口被遮挡”这类成功返回值会让检查重新交给图形线程，于是房间切换等连续多场战斗的测试偶尔在第二、三场开始时超时失败。现在任何成功返回值都按设备正常处理，只有失败的提交或检查才会再次询问显卡驱动。
- 测试回执 `quiet-sockets.ini` 新增直接回答与询问驱动的次数，以及非 D3D_OK 成功返回值的次数，便于排查。
- 只影响开发用的 GameTest 客户端 Mod（0.1.30），不影响玩家运行的 Mod。

## English

GameTest fix: a native client on the hidden desktop no longer stalls on the graphics state check at a battle start.

- GameTest runs the client on a hidden desktop. While presents succeed, the test layer answers the graphics device's cooperative check itself instead of handing it to its graphics worker, because at a battle start a loader thread can hold the device lock while it waits for the game thread.
- Before, that direct answer applied only when the results were exactly D3D_OK. Success codes that are common on the hidden desktop, such as "window occluded", sent the check back to the graphics worker, so multi-battle tests such as room switching occasionally timed out at the start of the second or third battle. Any success code now counts as a healthy device; only a failed present or check asks the driver again.
- The test receipt `quiet-sockets.ini` adds how many checks were answered directly or by the driver, and how many success codes other than D3D_OK were seen.
- Affects only the developer GameTest client Mod (0.1.30); player Mods are unchanged.
