## 中文

修复对战 AI 在 TAB 面板中的剩余生命停在初始值、不随死亡更新的问题。

- AI 参与原生剩余生命刷新，死亡竞赛使用服务器实际扣命的成本计算显示。
- 保留真人、普通 NPC、卡组生命、复活及新对局的原生规则。
- 更新后重启游戏客户端生效。

## English

Fixes PvP AI remaining lives staying at their initial value on the TAB roster after deaths.

- Registered AI participate in the native life-display update. Death match uses the life cost actually charged by the server.
- Preserves native behavior for human players, ordinary NPCs, decks, respawns and new battles.
- Restart the game client after updating.
