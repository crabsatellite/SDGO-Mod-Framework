# SDGO Mod Manager 1.16.66

## 中文

- 对战 AI 脚下的可破坏地板塌陷后，如果正掉向死亡区、原版移动可用且附近有安全落点，AI 会尝试用剩余推进脱险。落地后继续正常交战。

## English

- PvP bots can use their remaining boost to escape a fall after a destructible platform collapses. The bot attempts the move when native controls are available and collision checks find a supported landing outside the death zone. It resumes normal combat after landing.
