# SDGO Mod Manager 1.16.63

## 中文

- OC、G改 1.0 和 G改 2.0 使用各自保存的强化数据，战斗按照房间体系加载。房主和加入者都能看到当前体系。
- 机体信息页以三栏展示机体、武器、技能、六维图和两套部件。可以查看自己的机库机体和其他玩家当前出击的机体。
- 终极强化页显示剩余点数和实际道具库存，兼容保留的成功卡与保护卡。已达 EX 的机体可以打开技能选择，消耗前需要确认。
- 机体外观客户端 0.2.8 修复贴花纹理和磨损遮罩清理导致的游戏退出卡住问题。

## English

- Battles use the saved OC, G1 or G2 loadout selected by the room. Hosts and guests can see the room’s system.
- The three-column unit card shows the model, weapons, skills, stat chart and both parts sets. View your own hangar units and other players’ current sortie units.
- The OC page shows remaining points and actual item stock, including retained success and protection coupons. Units at EX can open the ability selector and review the cost before confirming.
- Unit appearance client 0.2.8 fixes a game-exit hang during sticker and wear-mask cleanup.
