## 中文

OC 六维强化页面的切换修正（客户端 Mod OC 六维强化 0.6.4）。

- 从「强化部件」等其他标签点击「终极强化」时，「经验值转换」页的库存窗口不再从右侧滑出、与加点窗口重叠；机体下方的「经验值转换」「还原」按钮也不再闪现。OC 页面在菜单切换完成的同一帧出现。
- 通过 SDGO Mod Manager 或服主提供的客户端包或更新取得新版，更新后重启客户端；服务端 Mod 不变。

## English

Tab switching fix for the Over-custom page (client Mod Over-custom stats 0.6.4).

- Clicking 终极强化 from another tab such as 强化部件 no longer lets the 经验值转换 page's inventory slide in from the right over the allocation window, and the 经验值转换 and 还原 buttons under the unit no longer flash. The OC page shows in the frame the menu finishes switching.
- Update through SDGO Mod Manager or the server owner's client package or update, then restart the client; the server Mods are unchanged.
