## 中文

- 新增王牌驾驶员：对战 AI 与我方任务僚机默认各有 5% 概率成为王牌；每档设为 0% 即可关闭。
- Easy 王牌使用 Normal AI，Normal 王牌使用 Hard AI；Hard 王牌仍用 Hard AI，默认生命 1.2 倍。可按基础难度分别配置概率、AI 档位、生命与普通攻击倍率，必杀不额外增强。
- 进入战斗后，原生头顶名称牌和 TAB 名单显示驾驶员名，保留原版字体和队伍颜色；复活、换机与原生形态初始化后仍保持同一驾驶员身份和服务端生命上限。
- 开战时沿用原生聊天栏，有王牌的队伍各提示一次“出现了王牌驾驶员”，不公开姓名或机体；任务显示“我方出现了王牌驾驶员！”。无需 AI 对话服务。
- 公开基础驾驶员表随程序发布。自定义机体可选导入驾驶员条目，服主覆盖表与个人名单保持在各自内容或服务端配置中。

## English

- Added Ace pilots for PvP AI and friendly mission wingmen, with a default 5% chance per AI. Set a tier to 0% to disable its selection.
- Easy Aces use Normal AI; Normal Aces use Hard AI. Hard Aces remain Hard with 1.2× health by default. Configure chance, effective AI, health and ordinary attack separately per base tier; specials receive no extra multiplier.
- Native overhead nameplates and TAB rosters show pilot names with the game's original font and team colours. Identity and authoritative maximum health survive respawns, deck changes and native form initialization.
- The native battle chat announces each team containing an Ace once without naming pilots or machines; missions announce a friendly Ace. No AI dialogue service is required.
- Public pilot identities ship with the programme. Custom-unit imports can carry optional pilot entries; owner overlays and personal rosters stay in the appropriate owner content or server settings.
