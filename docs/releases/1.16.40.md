## 中文

修复商城商品详情闪退和好友机体赠送入口消失的问题。

- 商城汉化正确保留原生面板对象及两个参数，修复自定义商品显示开启时进入详情或悬停提示的无效对象访问。
- 其他 Mod 的扩展回复不再覆盖赠送能力；普通赠送协议回复仍能更新或撤销权限。
- 后台机体详情读取忙碌或失败不再隐藏好友赠送入口；选择机体后的完整详情、资格预检、确认和不确定结果拦截保持有效。
- 更新后重启客户端生效。好友赠送仍默认关闭，由服主在 Manager 开启。

## English

Fixes crashes in shop product details and disappearing friend unit-gift entries.

- Shop localization preserves the native panel owner and both arguments, preventing invalid-object access when custom product display is active.
- Extension replies from other Mods no longer overwrite negotiated gift capabilities. Ordinary gift replies can still update or revoke permission.
- Busy or failed background detail reads no longer hide the friend-menu entry. Selected-unit detail, eligibility, confirmation and uncertain-outcome checks remain in effect.
- Restart the client after updating. Friend unit gifting remains off by default until enabled by the server owner in Manager.
