## 中文

导入任务增加原生护盾接触反馈，修复多人区域传送与僚机跟随。

- 活动范围同时覆盖真人、友军和敌方 NPC，并正确处理新刷出、复活和形态重建；过场演员保留原生演出。
- 内容作者可选配原生台词、局部网格与光圈，以及服务端确认的非致命接触扣血。默认关闭；接触最低保留 1 HP，不消耗生命或生成击破奖励。
- 各客户端使用本机真人的运行时 ID 和房间位置选择原生传送点；僚机跟随对应真人。
- 安装前校验边界声明和配套程序版本，客户端与服务端使用同源策略。

示范见地图与任务导入指南；具体关卡、素材与规则由服主内容包提供。

## English

Imported missions gain native shield-contact feedback and corrected multiplayer sector warps with wingman following.

- Movement bounds cover humans, friendly NPCs and enemies, including new spawns, respawns and rebuilt forms, while cinematic actors retain their native staging.
- Authors may enable native speech, a brief local grid/ring and server-confirmed nonlethal contact cost. This is off by default and retains at least 1 HP without spending lives or granting kills/rewards.
- Each client selects its authored destination by its real human's room position and passes the runtime object ID to native warp; wingmen follow the matching human.
- Installation validates boundary declarations and programme compatibility. Client and server receive the matching policy.

The map import guides include actual gameplay footage. Owners provide their own levels, artwork and rules through content packages.
