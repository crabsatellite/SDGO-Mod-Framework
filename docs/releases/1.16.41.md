## 中文

修复机库主展示在图形缓冲创建或上传失败后继续读取空资源的问题。

- 保护扩展到已核验的机库主展示入口；失败模型按原生所有权清理，调用方继续原有空模型与渲染锁释放分支。
- 列表格子继续使用原有二维缩略图回退，其他模型调用不继承该保护作用域。
- 原生故障注入已确认一次失败的源文件、根节点、候选模型均完成清理，后续展示可成功加载，并正常完成战斗、结算和大厅返回。
- 新增独立的主展示观察计数及默认关闭的 GameTest 回归选项；不随框架分发私有机体或素材。更新后重启客户端生效。

## English

Fixes null-resource reads after graphics-buffer creation or upload fails in the main hangar preview.

- Extends protection to the verified main-preview entry. Failed models retain native cleanup ownership, and the caller follows its existing empty-model and renderer-unlock path.
- Unit-list cells keep their native thumbnail fallback. Unrelated model calls do not inherit the guard scope.
- Native fault injection confirmed source, root and candidate cleanup, a later successful preview load, and a complete battle, settlement and lobby return.
- Adds separate main-preview counters and an opt-in GameTest regression. Private units and assets are not bundled. Restart the client after updating.
