## 中文

地图与任务导入增加原生大阶段完成提示和透明物件材质，改善分阶段任务的展示。

- 任务协议 v4 的 `section_clear` 可为小阶段关闭原生 Section Clear，在大阶段末尾保留提示；最终完成与失败继续使用原生结算。
- glTF `alphaMode: "BLEND"` 保留图片透明通道和材质透明度，同一贴图的不同透明度也能正确共存。
- `boundary_markings: "initial"` 可只绘制开局地面边界，避免后续区域标线提前出现在场景内；小地图和实际活动范围保留。
- 过场与终场的碰撞校验沿实际阶段链保留命名物件的显隐状态。地图编译采用 Manager 中实际选定的任务。

具体关卡、素材、NPC 血量和刷怪配置由服主内容包提供。原生画面对照见地图导入指南。

## English

Map and mission import adds native major-section completion cues and translucent props for phased missions.

- Mission protocol v4 `section_clear` hides the native Section Clear cue for intermediate phases and retains it at major section boundaries. Native completion and failure settlement remain available.
- glTF `alphaMode: "BLEND"` retains texture alpha and material opacity, including different opacity values sharing one texture.
- `boundary_markings: "initial"` paints only the opening ground boundary, keeping future-area markings out of the current scene while retaining radar and movement areas.
- Cinematic and ending collision validation retains named-object visibility along the actual phase chain. Compilation uses the missions selected in Manager.

Owners supply their own levels, artwork, NPC health and wave configuration through content packages. The map import guide includes a native-frame comparison.
