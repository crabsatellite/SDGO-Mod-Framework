## 中文

OC 六维强化的必杀技在战斗中生效（客户端 Mod OC 六维强化 0.6.5）。

- 修正：OC 房间里分配的必杀技点数此前不改变实际必杀伤害。必杀伤害由出招一方的客户端计算后上报，对战服务端只按上限截断，而 OC 对战服务端 Mod 加上的必杀追加值没有任何一方读取。现在本机打出必杀时按「必杀武器伤害 + OC 必杀点」计算，两者合计不超过 7950；随机浮动、多目标衰减和必杀觉醒倍率仍按原版计算。近战、远程和地图三类必杀都生效，只影响本机自己的必杀，可变形机体按当前形态的必杀武器计算。
- 攻击、防御和耐久照旧由对战服务端加到战斗机体上；推进力和机动力照旧由客户端加到本机机体上。
- 通过 SDGO Mod Manager 或服主提供的客户端包或更新取得新版，更新后重启客户端；服务端 Mod 不变。

## English

Over-custom special points now apply in battle (client Mod Over-custom stats 0.6.5).

- Fixed: special points allocated for OC rooms did not change the special damage dealt. The attacking client computes special damage and reports it, the Battle server only caps it, and nothing read the special bonus the OC Battle Mod added. The local player's special attacks now use "fatal weapon damage + OC special points", at most 7950 together; the random spread, the multi-target reduction and special awakening apply as in the original game. Melee, ranged and map specials all count; only the local player's own specials change, and a transformable unit uses its current form's special weapon.
- Attack, defense and HP are still added to the battle unit by the Battle server; speed and agility are still added to the local unit by the client.
- Update through SDGO Mod Manager or the server owner's client package or update, then restart the client; the server Mods are unchanged.
