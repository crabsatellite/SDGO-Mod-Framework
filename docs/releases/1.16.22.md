## 中文

机体一览汇总当前应用的获得方式，并补齐原版韩文说明与终极强化按钮的汉化。

- 终极强化页面各按钮状态中的「초 기 화」显示为「重置」，保留原生字体、颜色和按钮素材。
- 148 条原版活动获得说明显示为中文，保留日期和历史活动含义；自定义内容及追加的获得说明继续随内容包分发。
- 活动和自定义扭蛋列出实际机台号码；商城机体、设计图及副官设计图掉落提供相应来源。
- 设计图的合成关联与取得方式跟随已应用内容更新。各功能可选联动，缺少某个提供者时只省略其来源。

## English

The unit encyclopedia combines applied acquisition sources, alongside Chinese translations for stock Korean descriptions and the factory's Over Custom reset button.

- The spaced Korean reset caption displays as Chinese in each native button state, retaining its font, colors and artwork.
- Translates 148 stock acquisition descriptions while preserving historical dates and meaning. Custom content and appended acquisition hints remain owned by their content packages.
- Activity and custom capsule sources use visible machine numbers. Listed units and blueprints, plus adjutant blueprint drops, contribute their matching sources.
- Blueprint synthesis links and acquisition information follow applied content. Integrations are optional; missing providers omit only their own sources.
